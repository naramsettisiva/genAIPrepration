HealthConnect (sample) Telehealth Services

Virtual Primary Care: $0 copay for telehealth visits with in-network providers.
Available 24/7 through the HealthConnect (sample) app.

Virtual Mental Health: $0 copay for the first 10 therapy sessions per year,
then $25 per session. Includes therapy and psychiatry.

Prescription via Telehealth: Providers can prescribe medications during virtual
visits, sent directly to your preferred pharmacy.

How to Start a Telehealth Visit: Open the HealthConnect (sample) app, tap "Talk to a Doctor",
select your reason for visit, and you'll be connected to an available provider
within 15 minutes on average.

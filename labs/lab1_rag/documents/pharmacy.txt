HealthConnect (sample) Pharmacy and Prescription Guide

Prescription Refills: To refill a prescription, use the HealthConnect (sample) mobile app,
call the pharmacy directly, or use the auto-refill program. Refills can be
requested when 25% or less of the medication remains.

Generic Drugs: $10 copay for a 30-day supply.
Preferred Brand Drugs: $35 copay for a 30-day supply.
Non-Preferred Brand Drugs: $70 copay for a 30-day supply.
Specialty Drugs: 20% coinsurance, up to $250 per fill.

Mail Order Pharmacy: 90-day supplies available at 2.5x the 30-day copay,
saving money on maintenance medications.

Prior Authorization: Some medications require prior authorization from your
provider before the plan will cover them. This typically takes 2-3 business days.
